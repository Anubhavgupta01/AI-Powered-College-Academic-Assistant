VERIFIED HOSTEL INFORMATION
ABES states that its hostels include 6 boys' hostels and 2 girls' hostels. Room options include AC and non-AC accommodation and single, double and triple sharing. Facilities described by ABES include sports courts, table tennis, gym, laundry services, and mess/canteen facilities.

2026-27 ACCOMMODATION TERMS
- Room allotment is first-come, first-serve.
- AC rooms are allotted only upon full occupancy of the designated room.
- AC usage: 5:00 PM to 8:00 AM on working days; round-the-clock on holidays, subject to commercial power availability.
- Desert coolers are strictly prohibited in the Girls' Hostel; Boys' Hostel use is subject to college policy.
- Limited attached-bathroom rooms are available for an additional Rs. 10,000, shared equally among roommates.
- Hostel security is Rs. 5,000 refundable and applicable one time when opting for hostel.

REFUND ON HOSTEL WITHDRAWAL
Only Security, Mess & laundry charges for unutilized months are refundable on a pro rata basis.

HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION TERMS
- Room allotment is on a first-come, first-serve basis.
- AC rooms are allotted only upon full occupancy of the designated room.
- AC usage is permitted from 5:00 PM to 8:00 AM on working days and round-the-clock on holidays, subject to commercial power availability.
- Desert coolers are prohibited in the Girls' Hostel; in Boys' Hostel they may be used according to college policy.
- Limited rooms with attached bathrooms are available for an additional Rs. 10,000, shared equally among roommates.

ELIGIBILITY LIMITATION
The public ABES placement page available through search does not expose a complete student placement-eligibility policy (for example, a full list of CGPA, attendance, back-paper, discipline, or offer-participation conditions).

The ABES website's mandatory-disclosure/footer area references a placement/career section, and an external copy of a document titled "Placement Policy 2026-27" exists on Scribd. Because that copy is not an official ABES-hosted source, its detailed rules have intentionally NOT been copied into this official-source dataset.

DO NOT INVENT
Do not infer placement eligibility from placement statistics. For a production RAG system, obtain the official ABES/CCPD Placement Policy 2026-27 PDF/circular and ingest it as the authoritative eligibility document.

EVENT-INGESTION NOTES
============================================================

For the Prompt 5 temporal pipeline:
1. Parse [EVENT: YYYY-MM-DD] as event_date.
2. Treat events before the current date as historical/expired.
3. Treat events on or after the current date as upcoming.
4. Do not assign dates to undated club events.
5. When an official ABES notice later provides a date for an undated club event, add a new dated record rather than guessing.

No future-dated club event was included merely because a club page calls something "Past/Upcoming Events"; the official indexed content did not provide an exact future date.

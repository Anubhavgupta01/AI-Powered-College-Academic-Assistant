REFUND POLICY FOR NEWLY ADMITTED STUDENTS
In case of cancellation of admission, fee will be refunded as per UGC refund rules.

HOSTEL WITHDRAWAL
In case of withdrawal from hostel, only Security, Mess & laundry charges (for unutilized months) are refundable on a pro rata basis.

RELATED ABES FORM
ABES lists an "Application For Refund / Adjust Excess Amount (Form No. S 18)" and an "Application For Hostel Security Refund (Form No. S 19)" on its forms page.
